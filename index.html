Welcome to Korean Bites

Welcome to Korean Bites, where the flavors of Korea come alive! 🇰🇷
Step into a world of authentic Korean cuisine crafted with passion and tradition. From sizzling barbecues to comforting stews, every dish is a celebration of rich flavors and vibrant culture.

Explore Our Main Food Corner:

  • Kimchi Jjigae (김치찌개): Kimchi stew with tofu or tuna.
  • Bibimbap (비빔밥): Mixed rice with vegetables, egg, and gochujang (spicy red pepper paste).
  • Tteokbokki (떡볶이): Spicy rice cakes with fish cakes and boiled eggs.

Explore Our Rice and Noodles Corner:

  • Ramyeon (라면): Korean-style instant noodles.
  • Kimchi Fried Rice (김치볶음밥): Stir-fried rice with kimchi.

Side Dish:

  • Kimchi (김치): Fermented vegetables, typically napa cabbage or radish.
  • Kimbap (김밥): Seaweed rice rolls with various fillings.
